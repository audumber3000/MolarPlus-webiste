Before we dive into the list, here are the must-have features every Indian dental clinic should look for:

### Patient Management
Comprehensive digital patient records — dental history, treatment notes, X-rays, consent forms, and contact details — all in one place.

### Appointment Scheduling
A visual calendar with drag-and-drop rescheduling, automated SMS/WhatsApp reminders, and online booking capabilities.

### Billing and Payments
GST-compliant invoicing, multiple payment modes (UPI, cash, card), and insurance claim processing.

### Communication Tools
WhatsApp and SMS integrations for appointment reminders, treatment follow-ups, and feedback collection.

Revenue reports, patient flow analysis, treatment completion rates, and custom exports.

### Mobile Access
iOS and Android apps so you can manage your clinic on the go.
